# Military service and battlefield

Career and promotion logic lives in `MilitarySystem`; group combat, movement, morale, terrain, and tactical state live in `BattlefieldSystem`; the isometric tactical view and touch/mouse commands live in `BattlefieldPanel`. The mission catalog and rank thresholds are data in `MilitaryData.ts`.

The character begins in ordinary frontier service to preserve the existing opening. From the journal, the player may leave service when no assignment is active or enlist again. Missions require service. Promotions check reputation, days served, enemies driven off, completed assignments, service experience, leadership, and (at higher ranks) recorded tactical decisions. Missions and battle outcomes award experience; successful tactical commands award up to 20 experience per battle so repeating orders cannot be farmed. The player's current family leadership skill is synchronized to military advancement.

The current playable sample is the fictional 1299 frontier skirmish. It uses a dedicated 12×8 tactical map, passability and pathfinding, terrain cover and sight obstruction, elevations, bridge choke point, group orders, focused targets, morale, cohesion, retreat, and a report that updates the military career, settlement relationships, quest events, family chronicle, and the historical simulation timeline. Unit definitions restrict armored cavalry to 1320 and later. The composition is small group simulation rather than individual soldier simulation.

The battle reads the equipped weapon and armor, the player's current health and stamina, deterministic settlement weather, and one grain ration if carried. Rain and snow slow movement and reduce visibility and archer effectiveness. Missing rations lower friendly morale and reduce friendly archer damage. The period-appropriate bow uses a real arrow inventory stack, has longer reach, and spends one arrow per shot; bundles can be bought at the village market. The chosen weapon changes player reach and damage; attacks spend battle stamina. Incoming battlefield harm is applied to the ordinary player health system. The journal offers three days of rest to recover up to 30 health and restore stamina; condition is included in save data. Equipment does not determine the whole result: terrain, unit cohesion, morale, commands, range, and supply also matter.

`BattlefieldPanel` supports touch and pointer/tap map orders. It shows weather, ration state, starting fatigue, losses, morale, cohesion, unit intent, player health and stamina. Turn on debug mode for the tactical terrain and unit state; commands and their results are also kept in the military service record. Save schema 1.10.0 stores campaign and live battle state; the battlefield loader migrates earlier schema 1 states with safe defaults.

Current limits: the sample mission is still the only playable battlefield map. The catalog has yaya, archer, cavalry, militia, and period-locked armored cavalry group types, but there is not yet a convoy escort or settlement-defense mission, supply wagon entity, separate campaign role selection, or non-combat resolution for this battle. Do not describe those as implemented until a mission uses them.

Run `npm run test:battle` for combat, rank gates, morale, equipment, weather, supply, and save checks. Run `npm test` for the full suite.
