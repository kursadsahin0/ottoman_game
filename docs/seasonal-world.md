# Seasonal world and weather

`TimeManager` is the authoritative simulation clock. It advances the 30-day, 12-month calendar from March 1299, supports pause and configurable time scale, and accepts a saved `CalendarConfig` with month length and season boundaries. `VillageClock`, crop growth, markets, family aging, quests and travel all follow that game clock. Historical source dates remain attached to canonical historical events; the fictional simulation date is not used to rewrite those source dates.

Weather is deterministic from the simulation date and season. The village clock stores the current, previous and target conditions; the visual state transitions across six in-game hours, and transition state persists with the settlement save. It supports clear, cloudy, rain, fog, wind and winter snow. The scene uses a fixed small particle batch, soft color overlays and original procedural ambience. Rain, snow and wind slow movement; rain/snow/fog/wind alter farmer routines and market hours; snow, rain, fog and wind increase regional travel time. The weather UI remains unobstructed.

Crop definitions live in `CropData.ts` and currently include wheat, barley and oats. They specify planting seasons, growth duration, seed and harvest items, yield, price and water need. Farm growth is recomputed deterministically from the planted date and shared daily weather; soil moisture and quality are stored per plot, poor conditions can reduce yield, and healthy soil/crop conditions can modestly improve it. Hasan and Rukiye work hidden household plots only while their village schedule places them at the fields. They use the same `FarmSimulation` preparation, seed, weather growth and harvest rules as the player, with finite persisted household seed and grain inventories. Market grain prices reflect seasonal scarcity. Oats use the existing crop rendering fallback while the art set has dedicated wheat and barley stages.

Save schema 1.7.0 persists calendar configuration, farm condition and weather transition state; older saves use safe defaults. `npm run test:world` covers season/year boundaries, alternate calendar settings, crop growth and harvest, weather transitions and save restoration. `npm test` includes this suite.
